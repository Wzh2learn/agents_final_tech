# Paper Review Check

Use this skill for rigorous pre-submission or revision checks.

## Review Focus
- Core claim: is it clear, novel, and supported?
- Method: are assumptions, variables, and algorithm steps complete?
- Experiments: do baselines, metrics, splits, and ablations support the claims?
- Writing: are section transitions, terminology, and notation consistent?
- Risk: what would a skeptical reviewer attack first?

## Output Style
- Lead with the most important issues.
- Give concrete fixes, not generic advice.
- Distinguish blocking problems from polish.
- Preserve contradictions and uncertainty explicitly.
