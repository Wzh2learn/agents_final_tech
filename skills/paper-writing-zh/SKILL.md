# Paper Writing

Use this skill for academic paper drafting and revision.

## Workflow
- Identify the target venue, audience, contribution, and evidence level when available.
- Preserve technical meaning and uncertainty; do not inflate claims.
- Prefer clear section logic: problem, gap, method, evidence, limitation.
- For Chinese drafts, improve scholarly clarity without making the prose stiff or machine-like.
- For English drafts, prioritize concrete verbs, tight topic sentences, and consistent terminology.
- When editing, return either a revised passage or a compact change rationale, depending on the user request.

## Checks
- Contribution and novelty are explicit.
- Claims match experiments or cited evidence.
- Terms, symbols, and datasets are consistent.
- Limitations and assumptions are not hidden.
