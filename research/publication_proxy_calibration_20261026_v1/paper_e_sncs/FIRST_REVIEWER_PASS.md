# Substantive First-Reviewer Pass

- **Contribution boundary:** The contribution is a six-object measurement discipline instantiated in two existing audits; it is not a new evaluator, ranking algorithm, causal theory, or acceptance claim.
- **Closest work:** Measurement validity, reproducibility, tie-policy sensitivity, and source-identity auditing are adjacent. The bounded novelty is their explicit separation and paired case evidence, not a universal framework.
- **Post-hoc/clustered identity:** The FinRank identity correction is post-hoc v2 after a wrong-identity v1 implementation. Counts are clustered by canonical `text_sha1`, so 442 occurrences across 305 records are not independent samples and are not a second frozen application.
- **What the two cases changed:** They change the interpretation of flat/layered score agreement, show contract-relative harmfulness, and narrow the status of identity-sensitive and reproduction claims.
- **What remains unexplained:** OCR-like invisibility on the frozen surface, deployment effects of other tie policies, and global/metadata metric drift remain unresolved.
- **Reviewer recommendation:** Retain the checklist as proposed practice, keep all negative/stable results visible, and require owner verification before any submission action.
