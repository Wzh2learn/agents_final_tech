# Data and Artifact Availability

The two-case evidence is derived from frozen repository artifacts and public benchmark materials. The manuscript package includes the editable LaTeX/BibTeX source, rendered figure sources and manifests, this availability statement, and hashes in `MANIFEST.sha256`. The original ICAART package is preserved unchanged under `source_iccart_original.tex` and `original_iccart_reference.pdf`. Formal publication release, signatures, payment, and identity commitment are owner actions and are not performed here.
