## 4. Cross-asset cautions that must enter experiment design

1. **State recovery is not downstream use.** Keep separate: old-state validity,
   recovery of sufficient current facts/constraints, and action consumption.
   The V5 generated bundles sometimes dropped a positive current fact, so action
   failures cannot yet be assigned only to reasoning.
2. **Retrieval implementations are not interchangeable.** G2 BM25 strips stop
   words/short tokens; G3 retains them and indexes session/time prefixes.
   Coverage differences (G2 dim1 BOTH `0.430`, G3 `0.4675`) must not be
   attributed to the evaluated method.
3. **Oracle and hidden references are diagnostics.** STALE hidden fields,
   MA-CERD Gold/action sets, CodeUpdateArena hidden tests and Partridge
   parent-derived references must remain outside actor-visible information.
4. **Development exposure changes data identity.** Every current STALE 8/16 UID
   panel, Partridge six-object panel, CodeUpdateArena family set, VersiCode
   eight-task probe and scope 20 transitions is already analyzed. It can support
   development or audit, not fresh confirmation.
5. **Engineering checks are not scientific results.** Schema/JSON/test passes,
   MA-CERD fixture checks and PRM smoke completion establish executability only.
6. **A simple baseline can close a mechanism.** Exact cache + miss
   revalidation explains scope 30-vs-35; alias-aware static plus repository or
   behavior tests classifies the current Partridge panel 6/6. New work must
   require a failure or decision space those baselines cannot explain.
7. **Learning labels need task-specific qualification.** Q3 visibility
   exceptions, VGMC/DPO one-way synthetic preferences, PRM smoke licensing and
   STALE hidden outcomes cannot be silently promoted into new gold supervision.
