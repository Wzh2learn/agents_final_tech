## Scientific Gate

The two screens are:

- `P1_EVIDENCE_USE`: use the five existing ordinary original program-error
  candidates and any already-frozen natural failures to locate where complete
  semantic information is lost. Reference/scoring defects and unobservable
  units are not model errors.

  evidence-sufficiency and acquisition-cost research. Record-internal text-only
  retrieval remains a negative control, not corpus retrieval.

The previous no-go remains limited to constructed role tasks and the 389
record-internal text-only questions. Always-expand is an information ceiling,
not automatically a cost frontier. A candidate must improve answer/risk at
matched cost or reduce total cost at a predeclared answer/risk non-inferiority
margin.

P1 is preferred if at least two natural failures have complete semantic
references, deployable visible decision space and a residual not explained by
full-context restoration, fixed calculation, normalization, label/period rules
or existing neutral retry. P2 is preferred if P1 lacks qualified natural
errors and the table-text tasks have legally recoverable evidence and a real
cost frontier after strong baselines.

No candidate is forced. If neither passes, stop the model stage and continue
Paper E and thesis work.
