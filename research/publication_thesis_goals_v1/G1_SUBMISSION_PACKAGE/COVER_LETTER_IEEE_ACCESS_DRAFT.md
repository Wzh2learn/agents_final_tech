Dear IEEE Access Editor,

We submit *Visibility Is Not Detection: Auditing Measurement Boundaries in
Staged and Evidence-Ranking Evaluation* as a Research Article.

The manuscript addresses a measurement problem common to staged AI and
document-intelligence evaluations: final scores do not show whether a defect
was visible to the instrument, consumed by the decision predicate, or harmful
under the stated equivalence contract. We present a bounded two-case audit
using a frozen staged document evaluator and the FinRank evidence-ranking
benchmark. The audit separates six constructs that are often conflated:
visibility, detection, contract-relative harmfulness, score sensitivity,
decision stability and reproduction gap.

The paper is methodologically narrow but useful to evaluation authors. It
provides deterministic evidence, explicit post-hoc corrections, a reproducible
scope boundary and a proposed six-step review checklist. It does not claim a
universal theory, a validated universal method or a general failure rate.
