## Backup: IEEE Access

Official sources checked:

- `https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/`
- `https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/`
- `https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/preparing-your-article/`
- `https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/stages-of-peer-review/`
- `https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/post-acceptance-guide/`
- `https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/about/article-processing-charges/`

Verified facts:

- Rolling gold fully open-access, multidisciplinary journal covering IEEE
  fields, including computational and artificial intelligence.
- Required IEEE Access template in Word or LaTeX with an exactly matching PDF.
- All authors and biographies appear in source and PDF; the submitting author
  needs a public populated ORCID; 3-10 keywords are required.
- AI-generated text/content must be disclosed in Acknowledgments, identifying
  the system, affected sections and level of use.
- Simultaneous submission is prohibited.
- Review is single-anonymized with at least two independent reviews and binary
  accept/reject decisions.
- APC: US$2,160 plus applicable taxes. No page limit; more than 20 pages may
  trigger an initial editorial review.
- The site advertises a 4-6 week submission-to-publication target, but this is
  not a guarantee.
- After acceptance, final files and copyright precede APC payment. Early
  Access appears on IEEE Xplore within 2-3 days after final files and is
  considered published; proofs normally follow in 7-10 business days.

Decision: `BACKUP_CONDITIONAL_GO`. The rolling route is operationally useful,
but the APC and the school's acceptance of Early Access/DOI require explicit
Owner approval.
