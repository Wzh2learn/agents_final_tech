Dear ICAART 2027 Program Chairs,

We submit our manuscript, *Visibility Is Not Detection: Auditing Measurement
Boundaries in Staged and Evidence-Ranking Evaluation*, for consideration as a
Regular Paper.

The paper asks how evaluation conclusions should be interpreted when a defect
may be invisible to the instrument, visible but not consumed by a decision
rule, or harmless under the evaluation's equivalence contract. It reports a
bounded two-case measurement audit. The first case studies a frozen staged
document evaluator with controlled perturbations; the second audits the
FinRank evidence-ranking benchmark, including preregistered tie metrics and a
clearly labelled post-hoc identity correction.

The paper does not claim a universal validity theory or a general agent
benchmark defect. Its concrete contribution is to separate visibility,
detection, harmfulness, score sensitivity, decision stability and reproduction
gap in one reviewable reporting surface, and to provide a proposed checklist
for evaluator authors.
