source-isolation and reference-semantics defects without rewriting historical
evidence, then execute one real source-disjoint feedback-learning comparison
and one independent delivery-evidence experiment before the 2027-10 thesis
review deadline.

  ICAART 2027 and IJCNN 2027 with official facts, comparable papers, costs,
  timing, unknowns, one primary route and one sequential backup.

  blocker; owner-only actions are limited to named fields and actions.
- `V3_AUDIT_CORRECTION_RETURN.md` resolves the 5/24 and 3/24 table-UID overlap

  cost-matched baseline on table-disjoint development and confirmation groups.
- One C3 experiment uses fixed outputs and semantically distinguishing visible
  evidence, reporting final correct delivery, wrong delivery, coverage,
  unresolved/rejection and end-to-end cost.
- `RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_RETURN.md` gives G1/G2/G3/G4 status, evidence paths,
  budget deltas, failures, and the next experiment.
- Local and GB10 affected tests pass; JSON/hash checks pass; Git is synchronized
  without rewriting frozen or historical evidence.

- production services, production Memory, credentials, `:5050`, frozen hash
  manifests, frozen raw responses or historical score files;
- Paper E frozen evidence and old V3 raw results except by adding versioned
  corrections and clearly marked post-hoc analyses.

Stop if:

- more than five files outside this continuation package plus the existing
  Paper E package and research runners change;
- an existing passing affected test starts failing;
- source isolation cannot be established without relabelling previously seen
  data as new confirmation;
- external submission, payment, authorship or publication actions would be
  required without explicit Owner authorization.
