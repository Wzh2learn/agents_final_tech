> To illustrate how a comparison family affects interpretation, we additionally analyzed six researcher-generated retrieval runs under graded nDCG@10. The family comprised 15 unordered pairwise comparisons in each dataset; each was evaluated with a two-sided paired sign-flip test and Holm correction within that dataset and metric condition. For BGE-small-en-v1.5 versus BM25(1.2, 0.75), the observed mean differences were +0.05049 on SciFact and +0.03759 on NFCorpus. The corresponding sign-flip p-values were 0.00650 and 0.00125, and Holm-adjusted values were 0.06500 and 0.01500, respectively. Under this particular family and alpha 0.05, the former comparison does not meet the specified threshold while the latter does. The former result does not establish equality. These contrasts are exploratory, do not measure a person's decision, and do not imply a general property of dense retrieval.

**Required before use.** Independently inspect qrels conversion, sign-flip, Holm and family selection. The SciFact adjusted p is close to 0.05 and the Monte Carlo uncertainty matters. No multiplicity correction was applied across datasets or the four metric conditions; do not infer general robustness. The dense top-100 file lacks original scores, so dense tie prevalence cannot be determined. Avoid interpreting an unadjusted bootstrap-versus-adjusted-sign-flip contrast as solely caused by Holm correction. Do not replace the preserved five-system findings or overwrite the current review PDF.
