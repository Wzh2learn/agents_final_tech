# Stage E Threshold Visibility Analysis v1

Status: sensitivity analysis of frozen per-injection `visible_by_gamma` and table-level `detected_by_gamma`.

## 1. Two visibility definitions

`FACT`:

- The headline `96/118` uses absorption-based `visible_frozen`.
- The gamma grid uses `visible_by_gamma`, a threshold-conditioned score predicate.
- At `gamma=0.2`, threshold-conditioned visibility is `85/118`, not `96/118`.

`INFERENCE`: these are different measurement definitions. Comparing `85` with `96` as if one were a corrected value would be a construct error.

## 3. Monotonicity and failed predicate

`FACT`: injection visibility is non-increasing as gamma rises; the frozen artifact reports no monotonicity violations.

`FACT`: the literal family-range predicate fails at `gamma=0.7` and `0.9`. The failed predicate is retained rather than silently replaced with a family-specific or threshold-specific rescue.

`INFERENCE`: the grid demonstrates operating-point sensitivity and family-dependent attenuation. It does not show that a higher gamma is intrinsically more valid.

## 5. Claim boundary

- `FACT`: the curve is a sensitivity profile, not a calibration curve.
- `FACT`: no threshold was selected after inspecting a new outcome.
- `INFERENCE`: `0/13` across all thresholds is a structural negative-observability result for this construction.
- `UNVERIFIED`: transfer of the gamma curve to another corpus, parser, or production pipeline.
- `VALUE`: report all five operating points together so readers can see threshold dependence without treating one gamma as universally privileged.
